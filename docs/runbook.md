# Service Desk Runbook

Welcome to the service desk runbook. This document is the working set of notes for our support engineers.

## Chapter 1: Triage

- Read every incoming ticket in full before responding, and confirm the reporter and affected system.
- Classify each ticket by urgency and impact so that critical outages are escalated immediately.
- Check for duplicate or related tickets and link them before starting new work.
- Verify the ticket contains enough detail to act on; if not, ask focused clarifying questions up front.
- Assign the ticket to the correct queue or owner and note the next action and its due date.
